Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2024

Introduction

Welcome to ToolStation.world. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This privacy policy explains how we handle your information when you use our website and tools.

Information We Collect

ToolStation.world is designed with privacy in mind. All our tools run entirely in your browser:

  • We do not collect, store, or transmit your personal data
  • Your files, images, and text are processed locally on your device
  • No information is sent to our servers during tool usage
  • We may collect anonymous analytics data (page views, browser type) to improve our service

Cookies

We may use cookies for:

  • Essential website functionality
  • Anonymous analytics to understand usage patterns
  • Advertising purposes (Google AdSense)

You can control cookies through your browser settings.

Third-Party Services

We use third-party services that may collect information:

  • Google AdSense: For displaying advertisements
  • Analytics Services: For understanding website usage

Data Security

Since all tool processing happens in your browser and we don't collect your data, there's minimal security risk. Your files never leave your device when using our tools.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access any data we may have collected
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Opt out of analytics cookies
  • Disable advertising cookies

Children's Privacy

Our services are available to users of all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will notify users of any significant changes by posting the new policy on this page.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please contact us through our contact page.
