Frequently Asked Questions

What image formats can I convert to PDF?

You can convert any image format supported by browsers, including JPG, PNG, WebP, GIF, and more. The tool automatically handles all common image formats.

Will the image quality be preserved?

Yes! The conversion maintains the original image quality. The image is embedded in the PDF at its original resolution.

Can I convert multiple images to one PDF?

Currently, this tool converts one image per PDF. For multiple images in one PDF, you would need to combine them after conversion or use dedicated PDF merging tools.

What size will the PDF be?

The PDF is automatically sized to A4 format (standard document size), and the image is scaled to fit while maintaining its aspect ratio.

Is this tool completely free?

Yes, absolutely! You can convert as many images to PDF as you need, completely free, with no limits or watermarks.
