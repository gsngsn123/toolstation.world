Frequently Asked Questions

How do I find a YouTube video's thumbnail?

Simply paste the YouTube video URL or video ID into the input field and click "Find Thumbnails". The tool will extract all available thumbnail qualities for that video.

What thumbnail qualities are available?

YouTube provides thumbnails in multiple resolutions: Maximum Resolution (1920x1080), Standard Definition, High Quality, Medium Quality, and Default. Availability depends on the video.

Can I use YouTube thumbnails?

Thumbnails are copyrighted by the video creator. Use them only for legitimate purposes like promoting or sharing that specific video, or with permission from the creator.

What if the maximum resolution thumbnail doesn't work?

Not all videos have maximum resolution thumbnails. If the max resolution doesn't load, use one of the other available qualities like HD or Standard Definition.

Can I get thumbnails from private or unlisted videos?

This tool only works with public YouTube videos. Private or unlisted videos cannot be accessed unless you have the proper permissions.
