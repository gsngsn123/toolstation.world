Frequently Asked Questions

What can I encode in a QR code?

You can encode almost anything: URLs, plain text, email addresses, phone numbers, WiFi credentials, contact information, and more. QR codes can store up to several thousand characters.

How do I use the generated QR code?

Simply download the QR code image and use it wherever you need. You can print it on business cards, flyers, products, or use it digitally on websites and social media.

Will my QR code expire?

No, QR codes created with this tool never expire. Since the data is encoded directly in the image, it will work forever as long as you have the image.

What size should my QR code be?

For print materials, a minimum size of 2x2 cm is recommended. For digital use, 300x300 pixels works well. Larger is always better for scanning reliability.

Can I customize the QR code design?

This tool creates standard black and white QR codes. You can edit the downloaded image in graphic design software to add colors or incorporate it into your designs.
