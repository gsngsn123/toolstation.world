Frequently Asked Questions

How does the word counter work?

Our word counter analyzes your text in real-time as you type. It counts words by identifying text separated by spaces, counts all characters including spaces, and calculates sentences based on punctuation marks.

What is the reading time calculation based on?

Reading time is calculated based on the average reading speed of 200 words per minute. This is a standard metric used for estimating how long it takes to read a piece of text.

Can I use this for academic writing?

Yes! This tool is perfect for checking word counts for essays, articles, reports, and academic papers. It provides accurate word and character counts that meet most academic requirements.

Is my text saved or stored anywhere?

No, all processing happens in your browser. Your text is never sent to our servers or stored anywhere. Your privacy is completely protected.

Does this work offline?

Once the page is loaded, the word counter works entirely in your browser, so you can continue using it even if you lose internet connection.
