Frequently Asked Questions

How much can I compress my images?

Compression depends on the original image and quality setting. Typically, you can reduce file size by 50-80% with minimal visible quality loss at 80% quality setting.

What image formats are supported?

This tool supports all common image formats including JPG, PNG, WebP, and others. The output is always in JPG format for maximum compatibility.

Will compression affect image quality?

Some quality loss is inevitable with compression, but at 80% quality, most people can't notice the difference. You can adjust the quality slider to find the perfect balance.

Is my image uploaded to your servers?

No! All compression happens in your browser using HTML5 Canvas. Your images never leave your device, ensuring complete privacy.

Can I compress multiple images at once?

Currently, you can compress one image at a time. Simply repeat the process for each image you want to compress.
