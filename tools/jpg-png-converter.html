Frequently Asked Questions

What's the difference between JPG and PNG?

JPG uses lossy compression and is better for photos and images with many colors. PNG uses lossless compression, supports transparency, and is better for graphics, logos, and images requiring transparent backgrounds.

When should I use PNG instead of JPG?

Use PNG when you need transparency, for screenshots, graphics with text, logos, or when you want lossless quality. PNG is also better for images with sharp edges or limited colors.

When should I use JPG instead of PNG?

Use JPG for photographs, complex images with many colors, and when file size is important. JPG files are typically much smaller than PNG for photos.

Will I lose quality when converting?

Converting from PNG to JPG may result in some quality loss (lossy compression). Converting from JPG to PNG won't improve quality but will result in a larger file with the same quality.

Can I convert images with transparency?

Yes! When converting PNG with transparency to JPG, the transparent areas will be filled with a white background, as JPG doesn't support transparency.
