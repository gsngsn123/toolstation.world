Frequently Asked Questions

What is BMI?

BMI (Body Mass Index) is a measure that uses your height and weight to determine if your weight is in a healthy range. It's calculated by dividing weight by height squared.

What is a healthy BMI range?

A healthy BMI is typically between 18.5 and 24.9. Below 18.5 is considered underweight, 25-29.9 is overweight, and 30 or above is classified as obese.

Is BMI accurate for everyone?

BMI is a useful general indicator but has limitations. It doesn't account for muscle mass, bone density, age, or gender. Athletes or very muscular individuals may have high BMI despite being healthy.

Should I use metric or imperial units?

Use whichever you're more comfortable with. Metric uses kilograms and centimeters, while imperial uses pounds and inches. The calculator handles both automatically.

What should I do if my BMI is not in the healthy range?

Consult with a healthcare professional for personalized advice. They can help you develop a safe and effective plan for reaching and maintaining a healthy weight.
